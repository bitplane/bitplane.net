# Privacy

Canary: I do everything I can to protect your privacy.

## This website

This is a static website that is incapable of collecting deep data, it's just a
bunch of files in a folder. JavaScript is and always will be optional, and all
scripts are hosted locally.

However, the site is currently hosted on GitHub Pages and cache on Cloudflare,
which means Microsoft, Cloudflare and their government partners can see what you
are looking at. I don't have access to the logs, but I can see basic statistics
in the Cloudflare console.

The site is protected by SSL, but this does not protect you from spies. Military
intelligence from all competent nation states are collecting your traffic.

If you don't want third parties to know when and what things you're looking at,
you can download the source [here](https://github.com/bitplane/bitplane.net)
and browse it offline.
