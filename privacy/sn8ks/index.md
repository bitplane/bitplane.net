# sn8ks Privacy Policy

sn8ks is a game by Gareth Davidson.

It does not collect user data of any kind.

When installed from the Play store, Google collects crash reports and device
statistics, which are available to me on the Android Developer Console. These
do not contain personal information.

Downloads for other platforms including non-Google Android phones are available
at [sn8ks.bitplane.net](https://sn8ks.bitplane.net).
