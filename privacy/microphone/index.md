# Android Microphone Privacy Policy

Android Microphone is free software written by Gareth Davidson.

This application does not collect user data of any kind, and does not need
Internet access at all.

When installed from the Play store, Google collects crash reports and device
statistics, which are available to me on the Android Developer Console. These
do not contain personal information either.

Downloads for non-Google Android phones is available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/bitplane/Microphone), along with the full source
code. Feel free to audit the source, learn from it, or use it to make your own
microphone application.
