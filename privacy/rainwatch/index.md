# Rainwatch Privacy Policy

Rainwatch is an app by Gareth Davidson.

Rainwatch respects your privacy, whoever you are. It does not collect
information, but others might.

Your location is used to center the map, but this is not sent to anyone - it's
requested from a service that is likely already logging your location.

Rainwatch downloads weather data from Amazon Web Services. Weather data is
shared across the whole UK, so downloading it does not leak your location.

The app identifies itself as Rainwatch/version - it does not leak information
about your phone. Amazon can see your IP address when you use the app. If this
sort of thing bothers you then you should use a VPN (I do).

When installed via Google Play, Google collects crash reports and device
statistics, which are available to me on the Android Developer Console. These
do not contain any personal information, and are a Google thing not a Rainwatch
thing. The app works just fine on a non-Google Android device.
